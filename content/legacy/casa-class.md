# Casa (3 years to 6 years)

The Future Scholars Montessori Academy Casa program offers superior education to children from 3 through 6 years of age. The Violet room features a prepared environment in which the Casa children can excel in the Casa Curriculum areas of: Practical Life, Sensorial, Mathematics, Language, Culture (Geography, History, Science). Those five core areas are further enhanced by the introduction of Cultural awareness, Music and Creative movement.

The FSMA Casa Program takes advantage of the children's sensitive periods, those times when the child is especially receptive to learning of specific skills. The environment facilitates the independent learning of skills by giving the children freedom and a safe place to grow and learn.

FSMA teachers provide a nurturing, safe and comfortable atmosphere, while igniting the children's natural curiosity and desire to learn.

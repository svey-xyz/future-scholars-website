Recognition From The Mayor

## Our Mission

Future Scholars Montessori Academy in Ottawa strives to prepare children for a successful future by implementing Maria Montessori's Method.

## Our Vision

Future Scholars Montessori Academy is a contemporary Montessori school in Ottawa which enriches the traditional curriculum with Montessori materials. Young scholars are encouraged to grow academically, socially and emotionally. FSMA recognizes the importance of self discovery through play. To this end, our before and after care programs offer opportunities for creative play, art activities, supervised outdoor play, games and quiet time.

## Our Faculty

Each classroom is overseen by trained Montessori & ECE teachers.

# Admissions

## Application Process:

Future Scholars Montessori Academy considers a tour and a classroom observation essential for the application process to be initiated.

Please schedule a tour by calling our office at 613-244-FSMA (3762), or email us at info@futurescholarsmontessori.com

Following the tour, FSMA will accept the application and a non-refundable deposit.

Applications may be submitted at any time, however, preference is given by the earliest date received as well as siblings of enrolled students.

# FSMA Resources:

This page has a list of our favourite links. We hope you enjoy them!

- American Montessori Society — http://www.amshq.org — Future Scholars Montessori Academy follows the AMS principles.
- Maria Montessori's biography — http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Montessori — Find the condensed biography of Maria Montessori.
- MACTE - Montessori Accreditation Council for Teacher Education — http://www.macte.org — MACTE is the governing body of any teacher training center. Only the accredited teacher training centers should ever be considered by potential students.
- Inspirational Montessori Quotes — http://www.brainyquote.com/quotes/authors/m/maria_montessori.html
- Amazon list of Montessori books — http://www.amazon.com/Maria-Montessori/e/B001H6OKBG — These are great resources for those wishing to research more about the Montessori Method of education.
- Fun things to do in Ottawa — http://www.tripadvisor.ca/Attractions-g155004-Activities-Ottawa_Ontario.html — Enjoy your weekend.

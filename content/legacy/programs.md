# Programs

Future Scholars Montessori Academy offers Montessori education for children 6 months through 6 years by offering the following programs (curriculum enhancing & extracurricular below):

- Infants (6mo. - 18mo.) — Ratio 1:3
- Toddlers (18mo. - 3yrs.) — Ratio 1:5
- Casa (3yrs. - 6yrs.) — Ratio 1:8

Future Scholars Montessori Academy includes curriculum enhancing programs for its students. These include:

## Music

The music program teaches through active play. Children are invited to try instruments, listen to different kinds of music and emerge themselves in appreciation for sounds. FSMA offers this program to all its students, from the Infants through to Casa. We believe that music can be an integral part of learning. This particular well researched program adheres to our high standards and goes hand in hand with Maria Montessori's Method of Education.

Future Scholars Montessori Academy also offers before and after school care.
